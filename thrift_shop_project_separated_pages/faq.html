GOOD QUESTION

Before you thrift.

Quick answers to help you shop with confidence.

Are the clothes new or second-hand?

Thrift Shop focuses on pre-loved items. Each listing should include clear details about condition, size and any signs of wear.

How do I order an item?

Choose “Ask about this find” on a product and send us a message to confirm availability, size and collection or delivery options.

Can I reserve an item?

For this demo, reservations are handled by message. A real store should confirm its reservation policy before taking payment.

Do you deliver?

Delivery and collection arrangements can be agreed with the seller. Confirm the cost and location before placing an order.
